The Real Question Isn't "Which Font" — It's "How Many Voices?"

Every font you add is a new voice in your product. Right now you have 3 — that's already the sweet spot. Adding a 4th means heavier page loads (especially on Indian mid-range Android), more visual complexity, and a bigger maintenance burden. So the question is: does the AI chatbot deserve its own typographic voice, separate from the rest of the product?

The pattern is clear: Almost no AI product uses a different font for AI responses. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini all use the same font throughout. They differentiate AI from human through color, spacing, layout, and icons — not typography. The exception is monospace for code/data snippets, which you already handle with JetBrains Mono.

04 — My Recommendation
The Smart Way to Give AI Its Own Voice

Don't add a 4th font. Upgrade your 3rd font instead.

Replace JetBrains Mono with IBM Plex Mono — and expand its role. Right now your mono font only handles ICD codes and timestamps. With IBM Plex Mono, you can also use it for AI-specific moments: confidence scores, diagnosis labels, processing status text, and data points within AI responses. This gives the AI a subtle "precision voice" without adding font weight or breaking consistency.

The AI chatbot's main response text stays in Inter (your body font) — just like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini do. But the key data points within AI responses (the diagnosis name, the confidence percentage, the ICD code) switch to IBM Plex Mono, creating a distinctive "this is AI-computed data" feeling without making the whole chat hard to read.

06 — Why IBM Plex Mono Wins
Why This Font Specifically

Warmer Than JetBrains Mono

IBM Plex Mono has humanist touches — slightly rounded terminals, elegant italics inspired by the IBM Selectric typewriter. It feels "intelligent" not "cold." JetBrains Mono was designed purely for code editors; IBM Plex was designed for brand + code.

Perfect for Clinical Data

Monospace = every character same width = numbers align perfectly in tables. ICD codes like M47.816 look precise and trustworthy. This matters when clinicians are scanning data.

Signals "AI Computed This"

When users see monospace text for a diagnosis, they subconsciously read it as "a system generated this" rather than "a person typed this." That's exactly the trust signal you want: the AI is a precision tool, not guessing.

Free + Same Weight as JetBrains

Open source on Google Fonts. You're swapping one free monospace for another — no extra file weight. Same number of fonts loaded. Zero performance cost. Just a better choice for your specific context.

08 — Summary
The Final Font Stack
Aa Aa Aa

3 fonts. 3 voices. 0 extra weight.

Plus Jakarta Sans = the brand voice (headers, navigation, marketing)
Inter = the clinical voice (body text, notes, chat messages)
IBM Plex Mono = the AI/data voice (diagnoses, scores, codes, timestamps)

The AI doesn't need a completely different font for its chat messages — that would make the interface feel disjointed. Instead, IBM Plex Mono acts as a precision accent: appearing within AI responses for the specific data points that the AI computed, creating a subtle but powerful "this was intelligently analyzed" feeling.
