## 1. Global Physiotherapy Billing Models

### 1A. India (Primary Market)

**Billing Model:** Predominantly cash-pay / out-of-pocket

| Model | Details | Price Range |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Per Session | Flat fee per visit | INR 500-3,000 |
| Per Modality | Different rates by specialty | Basic: INR 699-1,500/hr, Neuro: INR 1,500-2,500/hr, Ortho: INR 1,200-2,500/hr, Sports: INR 1,500-2,000/hr |
| Session Packs | Bulk purchase with discount | 5 sessions: INR 3,000-7,500, 10 sessions: INR 6,000-15,000 |
| Home Visits | 30-40% premium over clinic | INR 800-2,000/session + INR 200-500 travel |
| Corporate Wellness | Employer-funded packages | Per-employee-per-month or per-session contracts |
| Post-Surgery Packages | 15-20 sessions/month | INR 15,000-30,000/month |

**Key Facts:**
- No standardized billing codes for physiotherapy in India
- Most clinics use basic accounting software (Vyapar, Tally) not designed for healthcare
- Session pack discounts range from 5-15% for bulk purchases
- Industry moving toward value-based and outcome-driven models but lacks technology infrastructure

### 1B. USA (CPT Code-Based)

**Billing Model:** Insurance-driven, highly codified CPT codes, governed by CMS and AMA

**Key CPT Codes for Physical Therapy:**

| Code | Service | Billing Type | 2026 Rate |
|------|---------|-------------|-----------|
| 97110 | Therapeutic exercises | Per 15 min (timed) | Varies by payer |
| 97112 | Neuromuscular re-education | Per 15 min (timed) | Varies |
| 97140 | Manual therapy | Per 15 min (timed) | $27.17 base |
| 97530 | Therapeutic activities | Per 15 min (timed) | Varies |
| 97161 | PT evaluation - Low complexity | Per session (untimed) | Varies |
| 97162 | PT evaluation - Moderate | Per session (untimed) | Varies |
| 97163 | PT evaluation - High complexity | Per session (untimed) | Varies |
| 97035 | Ultrasound | Per 15 min (timed) | Varies |
| 97014 | Electrical stimulation (unattended) | Per session (untimed) | Varies |
| 97032 | Electrical stimulation (attended) | Per 15 min (timed) | Varies |

**Key Modifiers:**
- **GP** - Services part of physical therapy management
- **KX** - Medically necessary therapy exceeding $2,410 threshold
- **59** - Distinct procedural service

**Critical Rules:**
- **8-Minute Rule:** Minimum 8 minutes of service required for a single unit
- Cannot bill for two deep-heat modalities on same area in same visit
- If two modalities administered simultaneously, bill only for the primary one
- 2026 CMS conversion factor: **$33.40** (3.26% increase over 2025)

**Billing Challenges (US):**
- 80% of medical bills contain errors
- 42% of claims denied due to coding issues
- Single denied claim costs $20-$50 to process
- Proper coding increases reimbursement by 15% and reduces denials by 30% (APTA)

### 1C. Australia (Medicare / MBS)

**Billing Model:** Medicare rebates through Enhanced Primary Care program

| Item | Details | Rebate |
|------|---------|--------|
| MBS Item 10960 | Core physio item for chronic conditions | $61.80/session (85% of $72.65 schedule fee) |
| Session limit | Maximum 5 physio services per calendar year | Shared across allied health |
| MBS 93000 | Telehealth (video) | Available for eligible services |
| MBS 93013 | Telehealth (phone) | Available for eligible services |

**Key Facts:**
- Requires GP referral and Chronic Disease Management Plan
- Most clinics charge above schedule fee, creating out-of-pocket "gap" payment
- Bulk billing clinics accept Medicare rebate (~$56-62) as full payment
- New GPCCMP system replaced GPMP from July 2025
- Safety net: After threshold, rebate increases to 85% of full charge

**Sources:**

### 1D. UK (NHS + Private)

**Billing Model:** NHS Payment Scheme (NHSPS) with Aligned Payment and Incentive (API) mechanism

**NHS Structure:**
- **Fixed element:** Guaranteed income reflecting efficient expected provider costs
- **Variable element:** Additional income based on actual activity (100% for elective in 2025/26)
- **Cost uplift:** 4.83% for 2025/26 (increased from 4.15%)
- Physiotherapy falls under Community Currencies Models - Short-Term Interventions
- NHS 10-Year Health Plan makes rehabilitation a core component

**Private physiotherapy** operates with direct patient payment or private insurance (Bupa, AXA Health)

**Sources:**

### 1E. Middle East (UAE & Saudi Arabia)

**UAE:**
- Mandatory health insurance for ALL residents since January 2025
- Dubai (DHA): Minimum 6 physiotherapy sessions/year covered
- Minimum annual limit: AED 150,000 for private health insurance claims
- Billing: Direct insurance billing or pay-and-claim model

**Saudi Arabia:**

- **Saudi Billing System V2 (SBS V2)**: Based on Australian Classification (ACHI) with ICD-10-AM coding
- Claims must be submitted within 45 calendar days
- Re-submission of rejected claims within 30 calendar days

## 3. India Physiotherapy Insurance Landscape

### 3A. Current State (2026)

**Coverage Status:** Limited but growing

| Insurance Type | Physio Coverage | Details |
|---------------|----------------|---------|
| Post-hospitalization | YES (most policies) | 30-60 days after discharge |
| In-patient | YES | During hospitalization |
| OPD (outpatient) | SOME insurers | Star Health, ICICI Lombard, Niva Bupa |
| Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) | Only with hospitalization | Standalone outpatient NOT covered |

**Insurers Offering OPD Physio Coverage:**
- **Star Health** - OPD cover includes physiotherapy
- **ICICI Lombard** - OPD benefits including physiotherapy
- **Niva Bupa** - Aspire Titanium+ offers up to INR 10,000 for in-person visits

**Waiting Periods:**
- Initial: 30 days
- OPD cover: Up to 2 years
- Pre-existing disease: Up to 3 years

**Common Exclusions:**
- Preventive care and alternative therapies
- External aids (wheelchairs, crutches, prosthetics)
- Diagnostic physiotherapy for posture correction

## 5. Strategic Billing Architecture

### 5A. Core Principle
**Billing type is per PATIENT, not per CLINIC.**

Same clinic can have:
```
Patient A (Walk-in)      → Cash per session: INR 800
Patient B (Pack buyer)   → Session pack: 10 for INR 7,000
Patient C (TCS employee) → Corporate: Bill to TCS monthly
Patient D (Star Health)  → Insurance: Submit claim to TPA
Patient E (Monthly plan) → Subscription: INR 4,000/month
```

### 5B. Three-Layer Architecture

**Layer 1: Service Catalog (Clinic-level)**
- Define services, modalities, pricing
- Configure session packs with quantities and discounts
- Set add-on modality prices
- Configure once, use everywhere

**Layer 2: Patient Billing Profile (Patient-level)**
- Self-pay (Cash/Card/UPI)
- Session Pack (prepaid, with remaining balance)
- Corporate (linked to employer account)
- Insurance (linked to policy, future-ready)
- Subscription (monthly membership)

**Layer 3: Visit Billing (Visit-level)**
- Auto-populated from service catalog
- Add/remove services performed
- Apply correct billing based on patient profile
- Generate invoice or submit claim

### 5C. UI Complexity Control

Clinics enable only what they use. Everything else is hidden.

| Clinic Type | Enabled Features | UI Complexity |
|-------------|-----------------|---------------|
| Solo practice, small town | Self-pay + Packs | 2 buttons |
| Multi-physio clinic, city | Self-pay + Packs + Modalities | Simple form |
| Corporate-tied clinic | Self-pay + Packs + Corporate | Medium |
| Premium multi-model | All models | Full (but organized) |

### 5D. Data Model (Backend - Supports Everything)

```
SERVICE CATALOG
├── id, clinic_id
├── service_name, service_type (SESSION|MODALITY|PACKAGE|SUBSCRIPTION)
├── base_price, duration_minutes
├── is_addon (boolean)
├── cpt_code (null for India, filled for USA)
├── icd_codes[] (for insurance markets)
├── gst_rate, hsn_code (India tax)
└── is_active

SESSION PACK
├── id, patient_id, clinic_id
├── pack_name, total_sessions, used_sessions, remaining_sessions
├── amount_paid, per_session_rate
├── covers_addons (boolean)
├── expires_at, status (ACTIVE|EXPIRED|EXHAUSTED)
└── condition_id (optional - condition-specific packs)

VISIT BILLING
├── id, visit_id, patient_id
├── billing_type (SELF_PAY|SESSION_PACK|CORPORATE|INSURANCE|SUBSCRIPTION)
├── line_items[] { service_id, service_name, quantity, unit_price, discount, total }
├── subtotal, tax (GST), total
├── session_pack_id (if pack deduction)
├── corporate_account_id (if corporate)
├── insurance_claim_id (if insurance, future)
├── payment_status (PAID|PENDING|PARTIAL)
├── payment_method (CASH|CARD|UPI|BANK_TRANSFER|INSURANCE)
└── invoice_id

CORPORATE ACCOUNT (Future-ready)
├── id, company_name, billing_contact
├── rate_card (JSON - custom rates per service)
├── session_limit_monthly, payment_terms
└── is_active

INSURANCE POLICY (Future-ready)
├── id, patient_id
├── provider, policy_number, coverage_type
├── sessions_approved, sessions_used
├── pre_auth_required, tpa_name
└── valid_until
```

## 8. Key Insight: The Billing Flexibility Moat

**Why this matters strategically:**

1. **Lock-in through billing:** Clinics that set up their billing in your system don't switch easily
2. **Network effects:** Corporate billing connects multiple clinics to employers
3. **Insurance readiness:** When IRDAI mandates digital claims, we're the only option
4. **Global scalability:** Same backend, different billing profiles per market
5. **Data advantage:** Billing data + clinical data = powerful analytics for outcomes research

**The Stripe parallel:**
- Stripe didn't just process payments - they built financial infrastructure
- HealUI shouldn't just bill sessions - we should build physiotherapy financial infrastructure
- When the market evolves (insurance, corporate, global), we're already there
